# 英语每日精读 · 2026-11-19

**标题**：We made GTA 6, the world’s biggest video game – we should have working conditions to match | Nestor Uriarte

## 全文对照

**背景**：2026年11月，Rockstar Games 发布《侠盗猎车手 VI》（GTA 6）。本文作者 Nestor Uriarte 是 Rockstar 爱丁堡工作室的动画程序员，他以第一人称在《卫报》评论版撰文，呼吁同事在工会认证投票中投赞成票。以下为文章可见正文（原文在此处截断于 “Continue reading...”）。

After the release of Grand Theft Auto VI this week, Rockstar employees like me can make history again. We need union recognition^{工会认证} to safeguard^{保障} our jobs.

When I first got the call offering me a job at Rockstar, I was thrilled^{激动不已}. I thought back to being a child and sneaking^{溜} into my brother’s room to grab the PlayStation Portable so I could secretly^{偷偷地} play Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories. The same kid who fell in love with Rockstar’s games back then now gets to spend every day helping to create those worlds as an animation programmer^{动画程序员} at the studio^{工作室} in Edinburgh.

This week, we will celebrate the culmination^{顶点、高潮} of more than a decade of effort^{努力}: the release of the most anticipated^{最受期待的} game of all time, Grand Theft Auto VI.

But our team has the opportunity^{机会} to make history a second time this month. Right now, the ballot^{投票} is open for Rockstar employees to decide whether we want the union^{工会} we have built with our colleagues^{同事} to get recognition^{认可}. As someone who cares deeply about our team, cares about what we do, and wants us to be able to keep delivering^{交付、推出} world-class games into the future, I’m voting yes.

## 生词与短语

### 高频核心词

| 单词 | 音标 | 词性与释义 |
|------|------|------------|
| recognition | /ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃn/ | n. 认可；正式承认（此处指工会获得法律承认，不是「认出」） |
| safeguard | /ˈseɪfɡɑːd/ | v. 保护，保障（比 protect 更正式，常接 jobs / rights / interests） |
| release | /rɪˈliːs/ | n. 发行，上映（此处指游戏发售，不是「释放」） |
| anticipate | /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪt/ | v. 期待，预料（most anticipated = 最受期待的，新闻高频搭配） |
| opportunity | /ˌɒpəˈtjuːnəti/ | n. 机会（固定搭配 have the opportunity to do sth，不用 chance 时更正式） |
| deliver | /dɪˈlɪvə/ | v. 交付；推出（此处指「持续推出作品」，不是「投递邮件」） |
| effort | /ˈefət/ | n. 努力（a decade of effort 十年心血；注意 effort 可数，常用 efforts） |

- **safeguard**：常搭配 safeguard jobs / safeguard our rights；不要与 guard（守卫）混用，safeguard 强调制度性保护。
- **anticipate**：be eagerly / widely anticipated 是新闻常见被动结构；错误用法：~~anticipate to do~~，正确为 anticipate doing sth。
- **deliver**：deliver results / deliver on a promise（兑现承诺）是商务与新闻高频搭配，注意 deliver on 的介词。

## 长难句解析

**句1**：The same kid who fell in love with Rockstar’s games back then now gets to spend every day helping to create those worlds as an animation programmer at the studio in Edinburgh.

- 主干：The same kid ... now gets to spend every day helping to create those worlds
- 定语从句：who fell in love with Rockstar’s games back then（修饰 the kid）
- 状语：as an animation programmer at the studio in Edinburgh（身份 + 地点，两个介词短语后置）
- 翻译：当年那个爱上 Rockstar 游戏的孩子，如今作为爱丁堡工作室的动画程序员，每天都能参与创造那些世界。
- 为什么难：主语 The same kid 与谓语 gets 之间插入 who 从句，且 back then 与 now 形成时间对照，读者容易在从句处丢失主干；句尾连续两个介词短语（as... / at... / in...）层层后置。

**句2**：Right now, the ballot is open for Rockstar employees to decide whether we want the union we have built with our colleagues to get recognition.

- 主干：the ballot is open
- 不定式状语：for Rockstar employees to decide whether...
- 宾语从句：whether we want the union ... to get recognition
- 定语从句：we have built with our colleagues（修饰 the union，省略了 that/which）
- 翻译：眼下投票通道已经开启，Rockstar 员工将决定：我们是否要让自己与同事共同建立的工会获得承认。
- 为什么难：the union 后同时跟定语从句和不定式 to get recognition，形成「名词 + 从句 + 不定式」的双重后置；want sth to do sth 结构中宾语被从句拉长，主干被切碎。

**句3**：As someone who cares deeply about our team, cares about what we do, and wants us to be able to keep delivering world-class games into the future, I’m voting yes.

- 主干：I’m voting yes
- 状语：As someone ...（as = 作为）
- 定语从句：who cares..., cares..., and wants...（三个并列谓语共用一个 who）
- 翻译：作为一个深切关心我们团队、关心我们所做之事、并希望我们未来能持续推出世界级游戏的人，我投赞成票。
- 为什么难：who 从句内含三个并列谓语（cares / cares / wants），中间还嵌套 what we do 宾语从句，状语长达两行，主句 I’m voting yes 被推到句末，是典型的「头重脚轻」结构。

## 核心内容摘要

- **Personal stake**: 作者以第一人称讲述自己从童年玩家到 Rockstar 动画程序员的经历，建立情感可信度。
- **Milestone release**: GTA 6 被视为十余年努力的 culmination，是「史上最受期待的游戏」。
- **Second chance at history**: 文章核心诉求是本月举行的工会认证投票（ballot / union recognition）。
- **Job protection**: 作者明确将工会承认与 safeguard our jobs 挂钩，这是其投票理由。
- **Call to action**: 结尾以 “I’m voting yes” 直接表态，属于典型的评论版呼吁式收束。
