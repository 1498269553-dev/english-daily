## 全文对照

UP 主艾伦·麦克法兰是剑桥大学社会人类学教授，这期视频回答网友提问：怎样辨别真爱，以及爱的能力是天生的还是需要学习的。视频语言为中文口语讲解，风格温和、夹叙夹议，带有学者式的自省。以下英文文本基于视频主题仿写，非视频原话。

Hello everyone. I am Alan Macfarlane, professor of social anthropology at the University of Cambridge. Today I want to answer a question from a viewer: how do you tell what true love is, and is the capacity to love something we are born with, or something we have to learn and work at?

Sir Walter Raleigh compared love to a deep well in one of his poems, with both joy and regret inside it. There are many kinds of love — for parents, for friends — and all of them can be genuine^{真挚的}. Here, though, I want to talk about the kind of love that makes you want to share your life with another person.

It is hard to define true love in advance^{事先}, but when it arrives you usually feel it. In my own experience, it may begin with physical^{身体的} attraction^{吸引}, though that attraction sometimes fades^{消退} once you have talked. What felt special to me was that she understood me and was considerate^{体贴的} towards me, and I felt the same about her. Gradually^{逐渐地} you notice something remarkable^{奇妙的}: when you make the other person happy, your own happiness increases^{增加}. Love is like a mirror — you give tenderness^{温柔}, and it comes back to you. Even when you are apart^{不在一起}, you talk to each other in your head and imagine^{想象} the next meeting.

As for whether love is innate^{天生的} or learned, I think it is both. We are naturally drawn to a particular person, but how to live with them requires practice^{实践}. When I was young, I saw many kinds of love in Shakespeare, in Jane Austen and in films: how people fall in love, how they face difficulties, and why they sometimes end in tragedy^{悲剧}. But reading those stories and entering a relationship yourself are two different things.

I have talked with some Chinese students. They told me that before university, parents and schools often worried that romance would distract^{使分心} them; yet at a freer university, they found they had few chances to learn how to build an intimate^{亲密的} relationship. I think being with another person is a little like playing the piano: you understand some principles^{道理}, and only after real practice do you gradually know how to do it.

If you are as lucky as I have been, and can love a partner who loves you for more than fifty years, you will know that true love really does exist^{存在}. I wish you good luck.

## 长难句解析

**句 1**：It is hard to define true love in advance, but when it arrives you usually feel it.

- 主干：It is hard to define true love ... but you usually feel it（but 连接两个并列分句）
- 从句：when it arrives 为时间状语从句，修饰后一分句
- 形式主语：It 为形式主语，真正主语是 to define true love in advance
- 中文翻译：事先给真爱下定义很难，但当它来临时，你通常能感觉到。
- 为什么难：形式主语 It 与后置不定式隔开，读者容易把 It 误当实义主语；but 前后两个分句结构不对称，前为「It is + adj + to do」，后为「主谓宾」。

**句 2**：What felt special to me was that she understood me and was considerate towards me, and I felt the same about her.

- 主干：What felt special to me was that ... , and I felt the same about her（and 连接两个并列分句）
- 主语从句：What felt special to me 作第一个分句的主语
- 表语从句：that she understood me and was considerate towards me 作 was 的表语，其中 understood 与 was 并列
- 中文翻译：让我感到特别的是，她理解我、体谅我，而我对她也有同样的感受。
- 为什么难：主语从句 + 表语从句双重嵌套，且表语从句内部还有并列谓语；the same 指代前文「理解与体谅」的感受，需要回指理解。

**句 3**：They told me that before university, parents and schools often worried that romance would distract them; yet at a freer university, they found they had few chances to learn how to build an intimate relationship.

- 主干：They told me that ... ; yet they found (that) ...
- 宾语从句：that before university ... would distract them 作 told me 的宾语
- 嵌入从句：that romance would distract them 作 worried 的宾语
- 省略 that：found 后接的宾语从句省略了 that
- 中文翻译：她们告诉我，上大学前，家长和学校常担心恋爱会让她们分心；然而到了更自由的大学，她们却发现自己没多少机会学习如何建立亲密关系。
- 为什么难：一个句子内三层从句嵌套（told me that → worried that），中间用分号转折，且 found 后省略 that，结构层次需要逐层剥离。

## 核心内容摘要

- **Defining true love**: 真爱难以事先定义，但来临时可被感知；它常始于外貌吸引，却要靠理解与体谅才能延续。
- **Love as reciprocity**: 爱像一面镜子，让对方快乐会反过来增加自己的快乐；即使分离，也会在想象中与对方交谈。
- **Innate vs. learned**: 被某人吸引是天生的，但如何与对方相处需要亲身实践，两者兼有。
- **Stories vs. reality**: 从莎士比亚、奥斯汀和电影里读到爱情，与真正进入一段关系是两回事。
- **Practice makes intimacy**: 与中国学生的交流显示，学业阶段对恋爱的压制可能让人缺少学习亲密关系的机会；相处如弹钢琴，需在实践中掌握。
