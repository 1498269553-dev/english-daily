## 全文对照

以下英文文本基于视频主题仿写，非视频原话。UP 主艾伦·麦克法兰是剑桥大学社会人类学教授，该视频回答网友提问：人类能否从历史中汲取教训？若不能，研究历史有何意义？语言风格为学术随笔式口语，用词平实、逻辑层层递进。

Hello everyone. I am Alan Macfarlane, Professor of Social Anthropology at the University of Cambridge. Today I want to address a question from an online viewer: Can human beings really learn from history? If we can, why do similar events keep happening? And if we cannot, what is the point of studying history at all?

As a historian and anthropologist, I have spent most of my life thinking about these questions. I understand the confusion. When we face war and conflict, people often ask whether the disasters of the past are not enough to teach us a lesson.

First, we need to distinguish between the exact repetition of historical events and the recurrence of similar ones. There is a saying: "History does not repeat itself, but it rhymes." Just as two different words can have a similar sound, different wars and the rise and fall of different empires may display similar patterns. But each has its own features, and the world does produce genuinely new things. We cannot simply apply the past to the present.

The value of studying history does not lie only in avoiding past mistakes. History is fascinating in its own right. It helps us understand how we came to be who we are, how civilizations developed, and why we find ourselves in our present situation.

But if history only tells the story of how we arrived at today, why would I write a letter to my granddaughter, discussing the world she will face? My answer is this: history may not offer precise lessons, but it can help us understand trends.

A trend is not an iron law. It is not "if A happens, B must follow," but rather "if A happens, B is more likely to follow." For example, power tends to corrupt people, yet some are able to use power constructively without being corrupted by it.

Our daily lives are built on this kind of predictability. When we book a taxi, we trust that the driver will arrive on time. When we go to college for lunch on Friday, we expect fish and chips. There are occasional exceptions, but without relatively stable expectations, life would be difficult to organize.

In my view, an important task for historians is to look beneath the surface of complex events for recurring deep patterns and trends, so as to provide a basis for understanding ourselves and judging the future. The history of wars of aggression is an important reference for judging the prospects of such wars: they tend to bring disaster to the aggressor and leave heavy casualties and grief for the attacked.

History cannot guarantee that we will never make mistakes again, but it can give us some awareness of certain consequences. This awareness is not an absolute prediction, but a well-grounded judgment.

## 长难句解析

**句 1**：First, we need to distinguish between the exact repetition of historical events and the recurrence of similar ones.

- 主干：we need to distinguish between A and B。
- A = the exact repetition of historical events；B = the recurrence of similar ones。
- ones 是代词，指代 events，避免重复。
- 中文：首先，我们需要区分历史事件的完全重演与类似事件的再次发生。
- 为什么难：distinguish between 后接两个并列名词短语，每个短语内部又有 of 结构作后置定语，读者容易在第一个 of 处断句，误以为 between 只带一个宾语。

**句 2**：In my view, an important task for historians is to look beneath the surface of complex events for recurring deep patterns and trends, so as to provide a basis for understanding ourselves and judging the future.

- 主干：an important task for historians is to look ... for ...。
- 介词短语 In my view 作状语；for historians 修饰 task。
- look beneath the surface of complex events 是方式状语，look for 的宾语是 recurring deep patterns and trends（被方式状语隔开）。
- so as to provide ... 作目的状语；understanding 与 judging 并列作 for 的宾语。
- 中文：在我看来，历史学家的一项重要任务，是从复杂事件的表象之下寻找反复出现的深层模式与趋势，从而为理解自身、判断未来提供依据。
- 为什么难：look 与它的宾语 look for 被一长串方式状语隔开，形成「动词—状语—宾语」的分离结构；句尾 so as to 后又有两个动名词并列，层次多。

**句 3**：The history of wars of aggression is an important reference for judging the prospects of such wars: they tend to bring disaster to the aggressor and leave heavy casualties and grief for the attacked.

- 主干：The history ... is an important reference。
- of wars of aggression 是两级 of 后置定语，层层修饰 history。
- 冒号后 they 指代 such wars，对前面作解释；tend to 后接 bring 与 leave 两个并列动词。
- the aggressor 与 the attacked 是「the + 形容词」表一类人。
- 中文：侵略战争的历史，是判断此类战争前景的重要参照：它们往往给侵略者带来灾难，给被侵略者留下惨重伤亡与悲痛。
- 为什么难：wars of aggression 中 aggression 是抽象名词作定语，中国学生易误读为「侵略的战争们」；冒号后的解释句主语 they 距离先行词较远，需要回看。

## 核心内容摘要

- **Distinguish repetition from recurrence**：文章首先区分「完全重演」与「类似事件再次发生」，引用「历史不会重演，但会押韵」说明相似模式不等于同一事件。
- **History's value beyond lessons**：研究历史不只为了避错，历史本身令人着迷，帮助我们理解自身与文明的由来。
- **Trends, not iron laws**：历史提供的是趋势而非铁律——「A 发生后 B 更可能出现」，而非「A 必然导致 B」。
- **Predictability in daily life**：日常生活建立在可预测性之上（等出租车、吃炸鱼薯条），偶有例外，但没有稳定预期生活难以安排。
- **Historians' task and limits**：历史学家的任务是从复杂表象中找出深层模式；历史不能保证不再犯错，但能让人对后果有所认识，形成有依据的判断。
