## 全文对照

UP 主是剑桥大学社会人类学教授艾伦·麦克法兰（Alan Macfarlane），本期视频回答一位网友的提问：一个人可能既善良又自私，我们该接受这些矛盾，还是设法消除？面对同样复杂的他人，又该如何理解与评判？他以亚当·斯密和蒲柏为线索，主张人是由相互矛盾的力量构成的活生生的悖论。语言风格为学术随笔式的口语讲解，夹引经典。以下英文文本基于视频主题仿写，非视频原话。

Hello everyone, I am Alan Macfarlane, professor of social anthropology at Cambridge. Today I want to answer a question from a viewer: a person may be kind and selfish at the same time, carrying many contradictions^{矛盾}. Should we accept these contradictions, or try to remove them? And how should we understand and judge other people who are just as complicated?

The simplest answer I have found comes from Adam Smith. He wrote *The Wealth of Nations*, and earlier *The Theory of Moral Sentiments*; he was a friend of Hume. Smith says the key to understanding ourselves and others is to recognize^{认识到} that we are all made up of contradictory forces.

On one side, we are also animals: to survive we must compete, struggle, and pursue^{追求} our own maximum advantage, thinking of ourselves first. When a person goes to the baker or the butcher, it is not because he likes them or wants to help them, but because it serves his own interest to buy well. This is a strong "self-love".

On the other side, there is another force, roughly balanced: as human beings we are sensitive^{敏感的} to one another, and we long for^{渴望} approval, love, friendship and warmth. Humans are animals that seek emotional bonds^{联结}; other animals also groom each other and show affection. We need confirmation^{肯定} from the responses of others. Smith calls it "the love of society".

The two forces are both balanced and contradictory. Our every action, every thought, every feeling is often some balance between the two.

From the Confucian view, at one end is self-love and selfishness, at the other is social love, altruism^{利他} and goodwill, and what is sought is the middle way. One end need not be completely removed, and cannot be. With no self-love at all, one cannot survive; with complete selfishness, one is also in danger, because life everywhere requires others, and pure selfishness makes one mean^{卑劣的} and greedy.

So we are living paradoxes^{悖论}, collections of contradictions. What reason thinks is right may not feel appropriate^{恰当的}; the needs of body, mind and spirit often push us in different directions. Rather than^{与其} simply removing painful contradictions, we should see the creative tension^{张力} in them — it is exactly this that makes us human.

Pope wrote in *An Essay on Man*: "Know then thyself", man is "half to rise, and half to fall; the only judge of truth, in endless error hurled^{被抛掷}; the glory, jest^{笑柄}, and riddle^{谜} of the world". We are this riddle of the world. Understand it, and enjoy it. Thank you.

## 长难句解析

**句 1**：When a person goes to the baker or the butcher, it is not because he likes them or wants to help them, but because it serves his own interest to buy well.

- 主干：`it is not because..., but because...`（强调原因的判断句，not...but... 并列两个 because 从句）
- 修饰：句首 `When...` 为时间状语从句；第二个 because 从句中 `it` 为形式主语，真正主语是 `to buy well`
- 翻译：一个人去面包师或肉贩那里，不是因为他喜欢他们或想帮他们，而是因为买得划算符合他自己的利益。
- 为什么难：`not because... but because...` 双从句结构拉长句子；`it serves his own interest to do sth` 中形式主语后置，中国学生容易找不到真正主语。

**句 2**：Rather than simply removing painful contradictions, we should see the creative tension in them — it is exactly this that makes us human.

- 主干：`we should see the creative tension`
- 修饰：句首 `Rather than...` 为介词短语作状语，后接动名词 `removing`；破折号后 `it is exactly this that...` 为强调句，强调主语 `this`
- 翻译：与其一味消除痛苦的矛盾，我们不如看到其中的创造性张力——正是它让我们成为人。
- 为什么难：`Rather than` 后接动名词而非不定式，是常见失分点；破折号后的强调句 `it is...that...` 与前面的 `this` 指代关系需还原。

**句 3**：What reason thinks is right may not feel appropriate; the needs of body, mind and spirit often push us in different directions.

- 主干：分号前 `What reason thinks is right may not feel appropriate`；分号后 `the needs... push us...`
- 修饰：`What reason thinks is right` 为主语从句，其中 `reason thinks` 又是插入的主谓结构
- 翻译：理性认为对的事，情感上未必觉得妥当；身体、思想与精神的需求，也常把我们推向不同方向。
- 为什么难：主语从句内再套主谓（What reason thinks is right），两层嵌套易读乱；`feel appropriate` 中 feel 为系动词，后接形容词。

## 核心内容摘要

- **Central question**: 一个人能否既善良又自私？面对自身与他人的矛盾，应接受还是消除。
- **Adam Smith's answer**: 人由两股相互矛盾又大致平衡的力量构成——「自爱」（self-love）与「社会之爱」（the love of society）。
- **Self-love**: 生存与竞争要求我们追求自身利益，如去面包师处买面包是为自己划算，而非出于善意。
- **Social love**: 人渴望认可、爱与友谊，需要他人回应中的肯定，这与动物的梳理亲昵同源。
- **Confucian middle way**: 两端不必也不能彻底消除，所求为中道；完全无自爱无法存活，完全自私则危险。
- **Conclusion**: 人是活生生的悖论，与其消除矛盾，不如看见其中的创造性张力——正是它使人成为人。
