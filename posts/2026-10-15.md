## 全文对照

**背景**：本文为《卫报》2026年10月11日对英国新浪潮乐队杜兰杜兰（Duran Duran）的专访。乐队成立于1978年，是80年代MTV时代的标志性流行乐队，全球唱片销量超过1亿张。2022年入驻摇滚名人堂。文章围绕他们即将在伦敦O2体育馆举办的万圣节演出，回顾了乐队的鼎盛时期、低谷岁月以及长久不衰的原因。以下为文章正文（素材截取至导语结束处，为自洽完整段落）。

They spent their heyday^{鼎盛时期} on yachts^{游艇}, then rolled up their sleeves^{撸起袖子（此处指埋头苦干）} in the wilderness years^{低谷期}. Ahead of a Halloween show at the O2, pop's great survivors^{幸存者（此处指经历风雨仍活跃的乐队）} explain their staying power^{持久力}.

In 2022, Duran Duran were inducted^{正式引入} into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame. It felt like a big deal, a sweet moment of acknowledgment^{认可} for the band's five-decades-long career, the 100m records sold, the string of bulletproof^{无懈可击的} hits: Save a Prayer, Rio, Hungry Like the Wolf, Girls on Film. They were inducted by actor Robert Downey Jr; Drew Barrymore, Naomi Campbell and Gwen Stefani were on hand^{在场} to pay heartfelt^{由衷的} tribute^{致敬} and, as keyboard player Nick Rhodes points out^{指出}, "It's a big national live TV broadcast in America that goes out to millions of people," so the band booked some warm-up^{暖场} shows.

"And it so happened that there was a date that came up in Las Vegas on 31 October," says Rhodes. "And I looked at everyone and said: 'Well, we can't do a normal show. Las Vegas on Halloween – surely that's the opportunity for a little bit of fun?'" They worked up^{精心准备} a set of suitably themed material, including covers^{翻唱} of Billie Eilish's Bury a Friend and Talking Heads' Psycho Killer. There were, of course, costumes^{服装} involved, because, as Rhodes drily^{冷幽默地} notes, "When have Duran Duran ever not dressed up^{盛装打扮}?"

**搭配与易错点**：
- `induct sb into sth`：把某人正式引入某组织/名人堂。不要与 `introduce`（介绍）混淆。
- `pay tribute to sb/sth`：向…致敬。介词是 `to`，不是 `for`。
- `a cover of ...`：…的翻唱版本。注意 `cover` 作名词时，常与 `of` 连用。
- `work up`：精心准备，逐步建立。不要与 `work out`（锻炼/解决）混淆。

## 长难句解析

**句子 1**：
They were inducted by actor Robert Downey Jr; Drew Barrymore, Naomi Campbell and Gwen Stefani were on hand to pay heartfelt tribute and, as keyboard player Nick Rhodes points out, "It's a big national live TV broadcast in America that goes out to millions of people," so the band booked some warm-up shows.

- **语法结构拆分**：
  - 分号前：主句 `They were inducted by actor Robert Downey Jr`（被动语态）。
  - 分号后：主句 `Drew Barrymore, Naomi Campbell and Gwen Stefani were on hand to pay heartfelt tribute`。
  - 插入语：`as keyboard player Nick Rhodes points out`（正如…指出），插入在引语前。
  - 引语：`"It's a big national live TV broadcast in America that goes out to millions of people"`，其中 `that goes out to...` 是定语从句修饰 `broadcast`。
  - 结果状语从句：`so the band booked some warm-up shows`。
- **中文翻译**：他们由演员小罗伯特·唐尼引入；德鲁·巴里摩尔、娜奥米·坎贝尔和格温·史蒂芬妮到场致以由衷的敬意，而且正如键盘手尼克·罗兹所指出的，“这是美国全国性的电视直播，会传达到数百万人”，所以乐队预订了一些暖场演出。
- **为什么难**：分号连接两个独立分句，中间插入 `as` 引导的评论性插入语，后面又嵌套引语和定语从句，最后用 `so` 引出结果，层次多，主干容易被插入成分掩盖。

**句子 2**：
"And it so happened that there was a date that came up in Las Vegas on 31 October," says Rhodes.

- **语法结构拆分**：
  - 主句：`says Rhodes`（引语后置）。
  - 引语内部：`it so happened that...` 是固定句型，`it` 为形式主语，`that` 引导主语从句。
  - 主语从句：`there was a date`，其中 `that came up in Las Vegas on 31 October` 是定语从句修饰 `date`。
- **中文翻译**：“碰巧10月31日在拉斯维加斯有一个档期，”罗兹说。
- **为什么难**：`it so happened that...` 是中国学生不常主动使用的句型；`that` 从句里又套了一个 `that` 定语从句，两个 `that` 功能不同，容易混淆。

**句子 3**：
There were, of course, costumes involved, because, as Rhodes drily notes, "When have Duran Duran ever not dressed up?"

- **语法结构拆分**：
  - 主句：`There were costumes involved`（`involved` 为后置定语，修饰 `costumes`）。
  - 插入语：`of course`。
  - 原因状语从句：`because...`，其中又插入 `as Rhodes drily notes`。
  - 引语：`"When have Duran Duran ever not dressed up?"` 为反问句，表达肯定含义（他们总是盛装打扮）。
- **中文翻译**：当然，还涉及服装，因为正如罗兹冷幽默地指出的：“杜兰杜兰什么时候不是盛装打扮过？”
- **为什么难**：`costumes involved` 是后置定语，不是被动语态；`because` 从句中又插入 `as` 引导的评论；反问句 `When have... ever not...` 形式否定、意义肯定，需要理解其修辞效果。
